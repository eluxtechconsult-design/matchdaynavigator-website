How MatchDay Navigator works

MatchDay Navigator provides neutral, event‑specific guidance to help fans arrive informed and prepared on matchday — without replacing official communications or venue operations.

What it is

MatchDay Navigator is a temporary information layer activated around major football events. It focuses on arrival timing, access routes, entry policies, and high‑level guidance relevant on matchday.

What it is not

It is not a ticketing platform, marketing tool, navigation app, or crowd‑management system. It does not track location or influence movement decisions.

How fans use it

Fans access information related to their host city and stadium. When available, matchday guidance can be delivered via official channels such as WhatsApp during the matchday window.

How it fits into event operations

The service complements existing city and stadium operations by reducing uncertainty and repetitive enquiries, while leaving full control with host authorities and venues.
