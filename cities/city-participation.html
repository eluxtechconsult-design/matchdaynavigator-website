City Participation

MatchDay Navigator supports host cities by delivering clear, event‑specific matchday guidance to fans before and during matchday.

Participating cities approve high‑level arrival guidance, policies, and indicative timings. Information is delivered to fans only during the matchday window.

The service complements existing city and venue operations, helping reduce uncertainty and repetitive enquiries without introducing new operational responsibilities.
