Hospitality Participation

Designed for hotels, fan zones, bars, and accommodation providers supporting major football events — helping guests arrive informed, prepared, and on time.

How hospitality locations participate

Hospitality partners participate by making official matchday guidance easily accessible to guests before they depart for the stadium.

This typically involves displaying a QR code or link in guest‑facing areas (reception, lobbies, digital welcome packs, or fan zones). When scanned, guests receive event‑specific arrival, access, and policy information via WhatsApp.

Participation does not give hospitality providers access to any guest data, does not require system integration, and does not introduce operational or legal responsibility.
