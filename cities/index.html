For Cities & Venues

MatchDay Navigator is temporary matchday guidance infrastructure designed to support cities, stadiums, and event operators during large‑scale live events.

The service provides fans with clear, event‑specific arrival, access, and policy information via WhatsApp — reducing confusion and repeated questions on matchday — while preserving full operational control for host authorities.

What cities and venues use MatchDay Navigator for

  • Reducing arrival uncertainty and congestion before kickoff
  • Reinforcing approved stadium policies (bags, access rules, timings)
  • Providing consistent guidance across hotels, transport hubs, and fan zones
  • Lowering the volume of repetitive questions handled by on‑site staff

How the service operates

  1. Fans opt in by scanning a QR code or receiving a link tied to a specific event
  2. Information is sent via WhatsApp only during the matchday window
  3. Messaging is limited to arrival guidance, access routes, and venue policies
  4. All rules and timings follow the guidance set by the city or venue

MatchDay Navigator does not direct crowd movement, issue instructions, or override official communications.

What we require from cities and venues

  • Permission to place or distribute QR codes
  • Confirmation of approved matchday policies
  • Indicative timings (kickoff and gate opening)
  • A named operational or fan‑experience contact

No technical integration, infrastructure changes, or on‑site staffing is required.
