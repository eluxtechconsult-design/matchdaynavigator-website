City Participation

MatchDay Navigator supports host cities by delivering consistent, event‑specific guidance to fans before and during matchday.

What this involves

Cities approve high‑level travel guidance, policies, and timings. The platform distributes this guidance via WhatsApp during the matchday window only.

What is not required

No IT integration, no control‑room access, no enforcement role, and no permanent staff involvement.
