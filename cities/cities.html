City Participation

MatchDay Navigator supports host cities by delivering clear, event‑specific matchday guidance to fans before and during matchday.

What this involves

Participating cities approve high‑level arrival guidance, policies, and indicative timings. This information is delivered to fans during the matchday window only.

The service operates alongside existing city and venue operations, helping reduce confusion and repetitive enquiries while preserving full operational control.

Next step

Cities interested in participating are invited to submit an official partnership enquiry to initiate onboarding and operational alignment.
