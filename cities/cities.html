For Cities & Venues

MatchDay Navigator is temporary matchday guidance infrastructure designed to support cities, stadiums, and event operators during large‑scale live sporting events.

The service provides fans with clear, event‑specific arrival, access, and policy information via WhatsApp, helping reduce confusion and repeated questions on matchday while preserving full operational control for host authorities.

What cities and venues use MatchDay Navigator for

  • Reducing arrival uncertainty and congestion before kickoff
  • Reinforcing approved stadium policies (bags, access rules, timings)
  • Providing consistent guidance across hotels, transport hubs, and fan zones
  • Lowering the volume of repetitive questions handled by on‑site staff

How the service operates

  1. Fans opt in by scanning a QR code or receiving a link related to a specific match or event
  2. Information is delivered via WhatsApp during the matchday window only
  3. Messaging is limited to arrival guidance, access routes, and venue policies
  4. All information follows the rules, timings, and guidance defined by the city or venue

MatchDay Navigator does not issue instructions, does not direct crowd movement, and does not override official communications or staff on site.

What we require from cities and venues

  • Permission to place or distribute QR codes
  • Confirmation of approved matchday policies (e.g. bag rules)
  • Indicative timings (kickoff time and gate opening windows)
  • A named point of contact for alignment (operations or fan experience)

No technical integration, infrastructure changes, or permanent staffing involvement is required.

What MatchDay Navigator does not do

  • No advertising or promotional messaging
  • No ticketing, resale, or access enforcement
  • No crowd control or security direction
  • No location tracking or behavioural profiling
  • No sale or reuse of participant data

Data, privacy, and governance

The service is opt‑in and event‑bound. Messages are delivered only during the matchday window and only for the relevant event.

Data is processed solely to deliver matchday guidance and is not sold, reused for marketing, or shared with third parties.

See the Privacy Policy for full details.

World Cup 2026 and multi‑city events

MatchDay Navigator is designed to scale across multiple host cities and venues while maintaining a consistent and neutral operating model.

Each city and venue retains its own autonomy and policies while benefiting from a shared matchday guidance framework for fans travelling between host locations.
