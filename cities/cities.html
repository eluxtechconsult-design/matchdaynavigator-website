City Participation

MatchDay Navigator supports host cities by delivering clear, event‑specific matchday guidance to fans before and during matchday — helping reduce arrival confusion while preserving full operational control.

What this involves

Participating cities approve high‑level matchday guidance such as arrival messaging, policies, and indicative timings. MatchDay Navigator distributes this guidance via WhatsApp during the event window only.

The service is designed to operate alongside existing city and stadium operations, reducing repetitive questions and helping fans arrive informed and prepared.

What is not required

No IT integration, no control‑room access, no enforcement role, and no permanent staffing involvement are required. Cities retain full authority over policies, rules, and decision‑making at all times.

Next step

Cities interested in participating are invited to submit an official partnership enquiry to initiate onboarding and operational alignment.

This allows the MatchDay Navigator coordination team to review the event context and respond with clear, role‑specific next steps and timelines.
