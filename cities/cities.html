Matchday guidance support for large‑scale events

A WhatsApp‑based guidance service that helps cities and venues reduce matchday friction by providing clear, multilingual information to visitors — without requiring a new app or account.

For cities, venues, and organisers

MatchDay Navigator is designed to complement existing city and venue operations by reducing avoidable informational pressure during large events.

The service focuses on arrival guidance, entry expectations, and last‑mile orientation through a channel most visitors already use.

How cities use MatchDay Navigator

Digital distribution

Official websites, fan guides, itineraries, and booking confirmations.

Operational support

Reduced repetitive questions and smoother flow at key touchpoints.

Multilingual visitors

Clear guidance for international audiences without added staff load.
