Matchday guidance support for large‑scale events

A WhatsApp‑based guidance service that helps cities and venues reduce matchday friction by providing clear, multilingual information to visitors — without requiring a new app or account.

For cities, venues, and event organisers

Large events place predictable pressure on arrival routes, entry points, and front‑line staff. In many cases, these challenges are informational rather than operational.

MatchDay Navigator provides a lightweight guidance layer that complements existing city and venue operations by helping visitors arrive better prepared and with clearer expectations.

How cities use MatchDay Navigator

Digital distribution

Links and QR codes can be shared via official websites, fan guides, itineraries, and booking confirmations.

Operational support

Reduces repetitive questions and last‑minute confusion at key touchpoints such as transport hubs and stadium approaches.

Multilingual visitors

Provides clear guidance for international audiences without adding additional staff or systems.

What cities and organisers gain

Fewer avoidable disputes, smoother crowd flow, reduced strain on staff and volunteers, and a calmer matchday experience for visitors.

The service is intentionally lightweight and adaptable to different event sizes and operational models.

Current status

MatchDay Navigator is currently running controlled pilots to understand real‑world usage patterns before larger deployments.

The approach is incremental and evidence‑based: pilot first, learn from real behaviour, and scale where appropriate.

There is no requirement for long‑term commitment at early stages.
