City Participation

MatchDay Navigator supports host cities by delivering clear, event‑specific matchday guidance to fans before and during matchday.

What this involves

Cities approve high‑level arrival guidance, policies, and timings. MatchDay Navigator distributes this information via WhatsApp during the matchday window only.

What is not required

No IT integration, no enforcement role, no control‑room access, and no permanent staff involvement are required.

Next step

Cities interested in participating are invited to submit an official partnership enquiry to initiate onboarding and operational alignment.
