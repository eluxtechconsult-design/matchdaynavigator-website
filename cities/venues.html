Designed to reduce repetitive matchday questions and support smooth entry, without replacing staff, signage, or operational control.

How MatchDay Navigator supports venues

Stadiums and event operators participate by confirming approved bag policies, access guidance, and gate‑opening windows. MatchDay Navigator delivers this information to fans ahead of arrival and during the matchday window only.

The service complements on‑site operations by ensuring fans arrive better informed, helping reduce congestion and repeated questions at entry points.
