Venue Participation

Designed to reduce repetitive matchday questions and support smooth entry, without replacing staff, signage, or operational control.

How MatchDay Navigator supports venues

Stadiums and event operators participate by confirming approved bag policies, access guidance, and gate‑opening windows. This information is then delivered to fans ahead of arrival and during the matchday window only.

By ensuring fans arrive better informed, MatchDay Navigator helps reduce congestion at entry points and lowers the volume of repetitive questions handled by on‑site staff.
