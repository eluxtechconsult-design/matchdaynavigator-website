How to get to SoFi Stadium

Getting to SoFi Stadium depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

In Los Angeles, arrival planning is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on route choice instead of route plus timing.

Traffic, final approach congestion, and stadium-area pressure can increase rapidly close to kick-off.

If you are staying near the stadium or in Westside / airport areas

Shorter journeys can still slow significantly as match traffic builds.

Even if the route appears simple, leaving too late can create avoidable delays.

If you are travelling from further across Los Angeles

Longer cross-city journeys require much more conservative planning.

Allow extra buffer for road congestion, parking flow, and final stadium access.

Transport options

  • Driving and parking require careful advance planning
  • Rideshare can help, but pickup and drop-off timing matters
  • Venue shuttles or structured transit options can reduce friction if available

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The final 60–90 minutes are usually the highest-risk period for delays

Traffic conditions in Los Angeles make late departures particularly risky on matchday.

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport preference
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)

MatchDay Navigator approach

MatchDay Navigator builds guidance using your starting point, transport mode, and arrival priorities to help estimate a more realistic matchday plan.

This is more useful than generic routing because it accounts for event-specific timing and crowd conditions.

Important note

Always follow official transport instructions, stadium staff guidance, and event-day updates.
