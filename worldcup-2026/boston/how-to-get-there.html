How to get to Gillette Stadium

Getting to Gillette Stadium depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

In the Boston region, arrival planning is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The most common mistake is focusing only on the route instead of route plus timing.

Regional travel distances and stadium access congestion can increase sharply close to kick-off.

If you are staying in Boston

Travel from the city often involves train or road-based journeys.

Even well-planned routes can take longer on matchday due to crowd volumes.

If you are travelling from outside the city

Longer journeys require conservative planning.

Allow extra time for traffic, transfers, and final stadium access.

Transport options

  • Event trains can provide structured access if available
  • Driving requires careful parking and timing planning
  • Rideshare availability may vary depending on peak demand

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The final 60–90 minutes are typically the most congested

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport method
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)

Important note
