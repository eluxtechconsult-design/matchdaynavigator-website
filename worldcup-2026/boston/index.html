Overview

Boston (Gillette Stadium) is one of the key host city environments for World Cup 2026.

Matchday in the Boston region is characterised by:
• longer regional journeys for many fans
• strong timing dependence around rail, road, and venue access
• congestion pressure near stadium approaches

Planning ahead is essential to reduce delays and improve your overall matchday experience.
