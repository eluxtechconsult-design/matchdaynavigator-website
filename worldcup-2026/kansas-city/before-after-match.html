Before and After the Match – Kansas City

Planning your time around the match improves your overall experience.

Before the match

The area around Arrowhead Stadium becomes busy well before kick-off. Arriving early helps reduce congestion and entry delays.

Food and timing

Eating too late can affect your entry timing. Planning ahead gives more flexibility before the match.

After the match

Congestion peaks immediately after full time. Waiting before leaving can help reduce delays.
