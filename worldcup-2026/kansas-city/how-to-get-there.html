How to get to Arrowhead Stadium

Plan your route and timing carefully to avoid matchday delays.

Key concept

The biggest mistake is focusing only on the route instead of when to leave.

From Kansas City

Travel is mostly car-based and congestion increases close to the stadium.

From further areas

Longer journeys require earlier departure to avoid delays.

Transport options

  • Driving is the most common option
  • Parking requires advance planning

Arrival timing

Arriving 2.5–3 hours before kick-off helps reduce congestion risk.
