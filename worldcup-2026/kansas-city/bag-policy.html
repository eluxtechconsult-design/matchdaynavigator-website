Arrowhead Stadium Bag Policy

Check bag rules before arriving to avoid delays at entry.

Why bag rules matter

Bag-related issues are one of the most common causes of delays when entering the stadium.

General guidance

  • Use small or clear bags when possible
  • Expect security screening
  • Avoid bringing unnecessary items

Matchday impact

Bag checks directly affect how long you spend in entry queues.

Practical recommendation

Travelling light is the simplest way to reduce friction and speed up entry.
