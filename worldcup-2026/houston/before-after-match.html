Before and After the Match – Houston

Planning your time around the match is an important part of your overall experience.

Before the match

The area around NRG Stadium becomes increasingly busy in the hours leading up to kick-off.

Traffic and arrival flow can slow down quickly, especially for fans arriving later.

Moving earlier helps reduce congestion risk and improves overall entry experience.

Food and timing

When you choose to eat affects your ability to enter the stadium on time.

Eating too late increases the risk of delays, while earlier planning provides more flexibility.

Preferences such as quieter environments or family-friendly timing may influence your plan.

After the match

The period immediately after full time is typically the most congested part of matchday.

Large volumes of fans leaving at the same time can impact traffic, rideshare availability, and exit times.

In some cases, waiting before leaving can help reduce overall delays.

Decision factors

• where you stay
• how you travelled
• whether you prioritise speed or comfort
