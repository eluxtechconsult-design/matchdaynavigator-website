How to get to NRG Stadium

Getting to NRG Stadium depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

Get matchday guidance on WhatsApp

Planning your arrival is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on how to get there instead of when to move.

If you are staying centrally

Road-based travel becomes slower closer to the stadium before kick-off.

If you are travelling from further away

Longer journeys require earlier departure to avoid delays.

Transport options

  • Driving is common but requires planning
  • Rideshare depends heavily on timing
  • Structured event transport can reduce friction

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The last 60–90 minutes are highly congested

Decision factors

  • your starting point
  • your transport preference
  • your arrival timing
  • personal constraints (for example travelling with family)
