NRG Stadium Bag Policy

Check bag rules before arriving to avoid delays at entry.

Why bag rules matter

Bag-related issues are one of the most common causes of entry delays at large events.

General guidance

  • Use small or clear bags when possible
  • Expect security screening at entry
  • Avoid carrying unnecessary items

Matchday impact

Bag checks directly affect how long you spend in queues before entering the stadium.

Practical recommendation

Travelling light is the simplest way to reduce friction and speed up entry.
