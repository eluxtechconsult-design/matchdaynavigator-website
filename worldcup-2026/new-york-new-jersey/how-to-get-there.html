How to get to MetLife Stadium

Getting to MetLife Stadium depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

Planning your arrival is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

In New York / New Jersey, the biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on how to get there instead of when to move.

Regional travel time, transfer points, and stadium access pressure all increase matchday friction.

If you are staying in Manhattan or central New York

Journeys from Manhattan often depend on timed rail and transfer coordination.

Even if the route appears simple, last-mile congestion can build quickly near the stadium approach.

If you are staying in New Jersey or arriving from the airport

Journeys from New Jersey hotels or airport corridors may require more conservative planning.

Allow additional time for road congestion, transfer delays, and final approach to the venue.

Transport options

  • Public transport can work well, but transfer timing matters
  • Driving and parking require more careful buffer planning
  • Rideshare can be convenient but may slow significantly near peak arrival windows

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The last 60–90 minutes are highly congested

Regional travel environments can make late departures much riskier than expected.

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport preference
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)

MatchDay Navigator approach

MatchDay Navigator builds guidance using your starting point, transport mode, and arrival priorities to help estimate a more realistic matchday plan.

This is more useful than generic routing because it accounts for event-specific timing and crowd conditions.

Important note

Always follow official transport instructions, stadium staff guidance, and event-day updates.
