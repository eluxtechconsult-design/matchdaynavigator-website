How to get to Lincoln Financial Field

Getting to Lincoln Financial Field depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

In Philadelphia, arrival planning is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The most common mistake is focusing only on how to get there instead of when to move.

Crowd density and transport pressure increase quickly as kick-off approaches.

If you are staying in central Philadelphia

The stadium district is accessible via public transport connections.

Even short journeys can slow down close to the stadium.

If you are travelling from outside the city

Longer journeys require conservative planning.

Allow extra time for transfers, station access, and final stadium entry.

Transport options

  • Public transport is often the most efficient option
  • Walking works well in the stadium district
  • Driving requires careful parking and timing planning

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The final 60–90 minutes are typically the most congested

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport method
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)
