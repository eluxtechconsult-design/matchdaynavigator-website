Before and After the Match – Atlanta

Planning your time around the match can significantly improve your overall experience.

Before the match

In Atlanta, the area around Mercedes-Benz Stadium becomes highly active before the match.

Strong public transport access means crowd density increases quickly near key stations and walking routes.

Moving early reduces congestion risk and helps ensure smooth stadium entry.

Food and timing

Deciding when to eat is an important part of matchday planning.

Eating too late can delay entry, while eating earlier helps align your arrival timing.

Preferences such as family-friendly options or avoiding crowded areas may influence your timing.

After the match

Immediately after full time, transport and walking routes become highly congested.

Large volumes of fans leaving at the same time can slow movement significantly.

Waiting briefly before leaving can sometimes reduce overall travel delays.

Decision factors

• where you stay
• transport mode
• comfort vs speed priority
