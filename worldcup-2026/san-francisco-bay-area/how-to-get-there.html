How to get to Levi’s Stadium

Plan your route and timing carefully to avoid matchday delays.

Key concept

The biggest mistake is focusing only on the route instead of when to leave.

From San Francisco or the wider Bay Area

Regional journeys can take longer than expected on matchday, especially when transfers or car travel are involved.

From nearby areas

Even shorter journeys can slow down close to the stadium as crowd density increases.

Transport options

  • Rail and public transport can help reduce road congestion
  • Driving requires careful parking and timing planning
  • Longer regional travel needs earlier departure

Arrival timing

Arrive 2.5–3 hours before kick-off to reduce congestion risk.
