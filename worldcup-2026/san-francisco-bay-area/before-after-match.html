Before and After the Match – San Francisco Bay Area

Planning your time around the match improves your overall experience.

Before the match

The area around Levi’s Stadium becomes increasingly busy before kick-off. Arriving early helps reduce congestion and entry delays.

Food and timing

Eating too late can affect your stadium entry timing. Planning earlier gives more flexibility before the match.

After the match

Congestion peaks immediately after full time. Waiting before leaving can help reduce delays.
