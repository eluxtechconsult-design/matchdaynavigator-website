How to get to AT&T Stadium

Getting to AT&T Stadium depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

In Dallas, arrival planning is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on route choice instead of route plus timing.

Road congestion, parking approach, and final stadium access can slow significantly close to kick-off.

If you are staying nearby

Shorter journeys can still become slower as stadium traffic increases.

Even if the route looks simple, leaving too late can create avoidable delays.

If you are travelling from further across the metro area

Longer journeys require more conservative planning.

Allow extra time for road congestion, parking flow, and final entry approach.

Transport options

  • Driving and parking require careful advance planning
  • Rideshare can work, but access timing matters
  • Shuttle or structured transport options can reduce friction if available

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The final 60–90 minutes are usually the highest-risk period for delays

Vehicle-based arrival environments make late departures particularly risky on matchday.

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport preference
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)

MatchDay Navigator approach

MatchDay Navigator builds guidance using your starting point, transport mode, and arrival priorities to help estimate a more realistic matchday plan.

This is more useful than generic routing because it accounts for event-specific timing and crowd conditions.

Important note

Always follow official transport instructions, stadium staff guidance, and event-day updates.
