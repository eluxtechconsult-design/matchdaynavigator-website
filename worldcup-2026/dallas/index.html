Overview

Dallas (AT&T Stadium) is one of the major host city environments for World Cup 2026.

Matchday in Dallas is characterised by:
• long vehicle-dependent journeys
• parking and road access pressure
• strong timing sensitivity close to kick-off

Planning ahead is essential to reduce congestion and make the stadium approach smoother.
