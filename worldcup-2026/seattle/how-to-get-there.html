How to get to Lumen Field

Getting to Lumen Field on matchday depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

If you're attending a World Cup match in Seattle, planning your arrival is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on how to get there, instead of when to move.

Congestion increases rapidly as kick-off approaches, even if you are close to the stadium.

If you are staying downtown

Many central Seattle locations allow partial or full walking access.

However, even short distances become slower close to kick-off due to crowd density.

If you are arriving from the airport or further away

Journeys from the airport or outer areas require more conservative planning.

You should allow additional time for transfers, waiting, and final stadium approach, especially during peak matchday periods.

Transport options

  • Public transport is generally the most efficient matchday option
  • Walking works well within central areas
  • Driving and rideshare should be planned carefully due to congestion

Arrival timing

  • Plan to arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The last 60–90 minutes before the match are the most congested

Most fans underestimate how early they need to start moving.
