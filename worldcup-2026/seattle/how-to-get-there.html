How to get to Lumen Field

Getting to Lumen Field depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

Get matchday guidance on WhatsApp

Planning your arrival is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on how to get there instead of when to move.

If you are staying downtown

Central locations allow walking or short transport, but crowd density increases close to kick-off.

If you are arriving from the airport

Airport journeys require additional time for transfers and matchday crowds.

Transport options

  • Public transport is usually the best option
  • Walking works well in central zones
  • Driving requires careful planning

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The last 60–90 minutes are highly congested

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport preference
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)
