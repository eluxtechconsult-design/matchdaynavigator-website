What to do before and after a World Cup match – Seattle

Planning your time around the match is just as important as getting to the stadium.

Before the match

  • Pioneer Square is a key gathering point
  • Arrive early to avoid crowd build-up

After the match

  • Expect congestion immediately after full time
  • Consider waiting before leaving the area

Movement after matches is one of the biggest friction points.
