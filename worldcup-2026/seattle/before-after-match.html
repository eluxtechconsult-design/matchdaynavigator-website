What to do before and after a World Cup match – Seattle

Planning your time around the match is just as important as getting there.

Before the match

  • Pioneer Square is a key gathering point
  • Bars and cafés fill up early
  • Arrive early to avoid last-minute congestion

After the match

  • Expect congestion immediately after full time
  • Restaurants and bars nearby remain busy
  • Consider waiting before leaving the area

Post-match movement is one of the biggest friction points for fans.
