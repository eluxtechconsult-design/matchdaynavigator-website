Before and After the Match – Toronto

Planning your time around the match can improve your overall experience.

Before the match

Movement timing is important, as crowd density increases quickly before kick-off.

Arriving early reduces congestion risk.

Food and timing

Eating too late can delay entry.

Eating earlier helps manage timing and reduces pressure before kick-off.

After the match

Congestion peaks immediately after full time.

Waiting before leaving can reduce travel delays.
