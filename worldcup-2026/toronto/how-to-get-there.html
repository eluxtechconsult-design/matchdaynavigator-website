How to get to BMO Field

Getting to BMO Field depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

In Toronto, arrival planning is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on route choice instead of route plus timing.

Even in a compact city, congestion builds quickly close to kick-off.

If you are staying downtown

Downtown Toronto offers strong public transport and walking options.

Moving too late can still create delays as crowds build.

If you are arriving from further away

Longer journeys require more conservative planning.

Allow time for transfers and final approach congestion.

Transport options

  • Public transport is often the best option
  • Walking works from central locations
  • Driving requires careful planning

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • Final 60–90 minutes are most congested
