How to get to Estadio Akron

Getting to Estadio Akron depends on where you are coming from, how you travel, and how early you want to arrive.

Planning your arrival is one of the most important parts of your matchday.

Key concept

The biggest mistake fans make is focusing only on how to get there instead of when to move.

If you are staying nearby

Shorter journeys can still slow down as congestion builds near the stadium before kick-off.

If you are travelling across Guadalajara

Longer journeys require additional time for traffic and final approach to the venue.

Transport options

  • Driving is common but requires planning
  • Rideshare depends heavily on timing
  • Walking may be possible from nearby areas

Arrival timing

  • Arrive 2.5 to 3 hours before kick-off
  • The last 60–90 minutes are highly congested

Decision factors

• your starting point
• your transport preference
• your arrival timing
• personal constraints (for example travelling with family)
