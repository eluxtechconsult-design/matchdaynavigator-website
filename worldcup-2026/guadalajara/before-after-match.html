Before and After the Match – Guadalajara

Planning your time around the match can significantly improve your overall experience.

Before the match

The area around Estadio Akron becomes increasingly active before kick-off.

Vehicle traffic and stadium approach routes become slower as more fans arrive.

Moving earlier helps reduce congestion risk and improves your entry experience.

Food and timing

Deciding when to eat is an important part of matchday planning.

Eating too late can delay entry, while earlier planning gives more flexibility before the match.

Preferences such as quieter settings or family-friendly timing may influence your approach.

After the match

Immediately after full time, congestion increases around exits and transport routes.

Large volumes of fans leaving at the same time can slow movement significantly.

In some cases, waiting before leaving can reduce overall delays.

Decision factors

• where you stay
• how you travelled
• whether you prioritise speed or comfort
