What MatchDay Navigator is

MatchDay Navigator is temporary, event‑specific matchday guidance infrastructure used to support host cities, stadiums, and partners during major football events — including the FIFA World Cup 2026.

It delivers clear, approved information to fans before and during matchday, focused on arrival timing, access routes, and venue policies. The service is opt‑in, time‑limited, and designed to operate at scale without introducing friction.

MatchDay Navigator complements existing city and stadium operations and helps ensure that fans arrive informed, prepared, and on time.

What MatchDay Navigator is not

MatchDay Navigator is not a marketing channel, advertising platform, or fan engagement app. It does not sell tickets, promote sponsors, or influence purchasing behaviour.

It does not direct crowd movement, enforce access, or replace venue staff, security teams, or official communications.

The service does not track location, profile users, or reuse data outside the scope of delivering matchday guidance tied to a specific event.
