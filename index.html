What MatchDay Navigator is

MatchDay Navigator is temporary matchday guidance infrastructure.
It provides fans with clear, event‑specific arrival, access, and policy information via WhatsApp, before and during major sporting events.

The service is opt‑in, informational, and time‑limited, and is designed to complement city and stadium operations — not replace them.

What this is

  • Event‑bound fan guidance
  • Opt‑in WhatsApp delivery
  • Operationally neutral information
  • Approved rules and timings

What this is not

  • Advertising or marketing
  • Crowd control or security
  • Ticketing or access enforcement
  • Location tracking or profiling
