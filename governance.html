Governance & Safety

MatchDay Navigator is designed with structured safeguards to ensure reliable, responsible, and context-aware matchday guidance.

Safety approach

The system focuses on providing practical matchday coordination guidance while avoiding unsafe, misleading, or speculative instructions.

It is designed to prioritise clarity, usefulness, and consistency in high-density event environments.

Controlled response model

MatchDay Navigator operates through a structured response framework rather than open-ended generation.

This includes:
• predefined response structures
• scenario-based routing logic
• contextual validation of user inputs

Guardrails and limitations

The system is intentionally limited in scope to matchday logistics guidance.

It does not provide:
• emergency instructions
• medical advice
• security directives
• official event decisions

Role within event ecosystems

MatchDay Navigator is designed to complement — not replace — the role of stadium authorities, cities, and event organisers.

Users are expected to follow all official guidance provided on-site and through official communication channels.

Consistency and reliability

By combining structured logic with contextual inputs, the system aims to deliver consistent and predictable outputs across similar scenarios.
