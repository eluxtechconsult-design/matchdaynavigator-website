About MatchDay Navigator

MatchDay Navigator is a real-time matchday coordination system designed to help fans, cities, and venues manage arrival, movement, and stadium experience at large-scale football events.

The problem

Major football events bring tens of thousands of people into the same location within a very short timeframe.

Fans face:
• uncertain arrival timing
• congestion risk
• unclear routing decisions
• poor coordination between transport, queues, and entry

Cities and venues face:
• crowd flow pressure
• transport bottlenecks
• safety constraints

The solution

MatchDay Navigator provides real-time, scenario-based matchday coordination.

It combines:
• user-specific context (hotel, transport, priorities)
• city and stadium profile data
• event-specific timing

to deliver precise, personalised guidance for each user’s matchday journey.

What makes it different

Unlike static guides or generic AI answers, MatchDay Navigator:
• adapts to each user’s exact situation
• integrates timing, transport, and congestion factors
• provides structured matchday planning, not just information

Vision

MatchDay Navigator aims to become a standard coordination layer for large-scale football events.

World Cup 2026 is the starting point.
