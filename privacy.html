Privacy Policy

MatchDay Navigator is designed to provide matchday guidance while respecting user privacy and minimising data use.

What we collect

MatchDay Navigator only uses the information necessary to provide matchday guidance.

This may include:
• messages you send
• optional location or starting point information
• preferences you choose to share

How information is used

Information is used solely to generate and improve matchday guidance.

It is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

What we do not do

MatchDay Navigator does not:
• sell personal data
• track users outside interactions
• build long-term personal profiles

Data handling and retention

Data is processed only as required to deliver responses and maintain service quality.

Where possible, data is minimised and not retained longer than necessary.

Relationship with official services

MatchDay Navigator operates independently of official ticketing systems, stadium operators, and public authorities.
