Service Overview

MatchDay Navigator provides live event guidance, routing information, and match‑day updates via WhatsApp. The service is designed to deliver clear, trusted information at scale, while minimizing data collection.

We process only the minimum amount of information necessary to operate the service effectively:

Personal data is used exclusively to:

MatchDay Navigator uses automated messaging systems to deliver timely responses at scale. These systems are designed to support information delivery and do not make autonomous decisions affecting users.
